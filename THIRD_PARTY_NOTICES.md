## Melodies

The 50 procedural arrangements use public-domain melodies or original compositions. They include
Brahms' *Wiegenlied* (1868), *Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star* (1806), *Schlafe, mein Prinzchen* (attr.
Flies, 1795), Schubert's *Wiegenlied* (1816), traditional lullabies, Welsh/French/German/English/
American folk songs, Japanese/Korean/Chinese/Irish/Scottish traditional tunes, and classical themes by
Beethoven, Mozart, Pachelbel, Bach, Debussy, Satie, Grieg and Schumann. The 18 `Dreams`/`Bonus`
tracks marked *Wurlitzer Dream Division* are original compositions. Note selections, tempos,
waveforms and envelopes are original to this project and released under the repository's MIT
licence.
