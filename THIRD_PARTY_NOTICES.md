## Melodies

The eight procedural arrangements use public-domain melodies: Brahms' *Wiegenlied* (1868), *Twinkle,
Twinkle, Little Star* (Jane Taylor, 1806), *Schlafe, mein Prinzchen* (attr. Flies, 1795), Schubert's
*Wiegenlied* (1816), *Rock-a-bye Baby* (traditional), *Hush, Little Baby* (traditional), *Frère
Jacques* (traditional) and *Golden Slumbers* (traditional). The note selections, tempos, waveforms
and envelopes are original to this project.
