# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a calm, welcoming place for anyone who likes sleep sounds, 3D graphics,
old machines, or all three. We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, specific and patient. Assume good intent.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Respect licences, attribution and public-domain status.
- Ask before sharing someone else's recordings, images or personal data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory comments, sexualised attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing
private information without permission, or sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any project space. Report concerns to the
maintainer via the contact route in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers may remove comments, close threads or block accounts as needed.
