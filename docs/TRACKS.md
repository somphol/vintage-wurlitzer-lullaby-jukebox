# Track catalogue

82 records: 50 synthesized music-box arrangements across A–G, and 32 real public-domain performances across H–K.

**Licensing.** Every synth track is an original arrangement of a public-domain melody or an original composition by Wurlitzer Dream Division (`rights: public-domain | original`). Every archive track is marked **Public domain** on its Wikimedia Commons page. No copyrighted recording or lyric is redistributed; the manifest contains URLs, not audio files.
