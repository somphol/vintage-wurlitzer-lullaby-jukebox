How to play the bubbler

  1. Pick a strip. Click any paper title strip in the rack, or press a letter then a number on the chrome keypad. A-1 through G-2 are synthesized music-box lullabies. H-1 through K-8 are real, public-domain archive performances across four collections.
  2. Feed the machine. Free Play is on by default. Turn it off and the jukebox needs a quarter for each selection; the coin drops into the slot in 3D.
  3. Shape the night. Turn the four chrome knobs for master volume, bass, treble and ambient mix. Blend rain, waves and crickets underneath the music.
  4. Set the timer. Choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes. The last two minutes fade smoothly to silence, then the cabinet lights go dark.
